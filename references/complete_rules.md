# War Room — Complete Rules Reference (2nd Edition, 2025 Update)

Based on the official rulebook by Larry Harris / Nightingale Games, with FAQ & Errata (v2, 2023) integrated.
This document is a structured reference for app development — not a replacement for the official rulebook.

---

## Objective

**Standard:** Seven Nations struggle to control key territories and critical resources before stress and devastation drain their ability to wage war. Unless otherwise stated in the chosen scenario:

- **Axis wins** by controlling **2 of 3** Allied capitals: *Eastern United States*, *Great Britain*, or *Moscow*.
- **Allies win** by controlling **both** Axis capitals: *Greater Germany* and *Japan*.
- Italy and China play a lesser role (not victory capitals).
- A Collapsed Nation whose Capital is NOT occupied by any Units of an ally counts as having its Capital captured for victory purposes.

**Limited (6-round variant):** Score 1 point per Zone beyond White on the Homeland Status Track. Orange Zone = 3 points, Collapsed Nation = 6 points. Lower total stress wins. Subtract Medals/Civilian Goods to break ties.

## Game Components

### Resources and Tracking

- **7 Resource Charts** with **28 color-coded pegs**: Oil (red), Iron (blue), OSR (yellow). White peg for tracking above 20. Resources are public information.
- **7 Pads of O&P Charts** (Operations & Production): secret orders and purchases each round.
- **131 Territory Cards**: double-sided. Front = normal income. Reverse (red/white stripes) = Embattled (reduced income).
- **11 Card Holders**: 7 nation-specific + 4 blank extras.

### Boards

- **1 World Map**: includes Turn Order Track and Homeland Status Track near the polar area.
- **1 Morale Board**: gold side for Global War and Eastern Front; silver side for War in Europe, War in the Pacific, and North Africa. Contains the Casualty List, Stress Track, Conversion Chart, and multiplying factors.
- **2 Battle-Boards**: Land/Air on one side, Sea/Air on reverse. Second board for simultaneous battles.
- **2 Dry-Erase Quick Battle Charts**: red (Axis) and blue (Allied) for optional Quick Battle rules.

### Units

**Land Units:**
- 155 Infantry, 90 Artillery, 65 Armor

**Air Units:**
- 80 Fighters, 30 Bombers

**Naval Units:**
- 25 Submarines, 35 Cruisers, 15 Aircraft Carriers, 15 Battleships

**Special Units:**
- 16 Carrier Fighters (double-sided: red = Axis, blue = Allied). No Command Token or written orders needed. Launch after all movement orders.

**Multiplier Units:**
- 6 Land, 6 Air, 6 Naval multipliers. White tokens representing 3 of whatever Unit Type is directly below them.

Units are not piece-limited — use multipliers as needed.

### Command Tokens

- **Land Commands** (square), **Air Commands** (circle), **Naval Commands** (elongated hexagon).
- Command ID: large number on black background. 1–9 for Naval, 11–19 for Air, 20+ for Land.
- **Command Stack**: a stack of Units capped by a Command Token. **Max 8 Units per Command.**
- Commands may NOT be disbanded. Commands are limited to what is shown on each Nation Storage Tray.

### Tokens

- **30 Hotspot Markers**: front = Active (needs resolution this round). Back = Embattled (ongoing conflict, red/blue).
- **30 Medals/Civilian Goods**: Medal side = battle achievements. Civilian Goods side = purchased with resources, cancel stress.
- **25 Bomb Tokens/Industry Tokens**: Bomb side = infrastructure damage. Industry side = marks units under construction.
- **20 Arrow Tags**: red = Axis, blue = Allied. Mark Air Commands that moved/fought (must land in Phase 5). Also denote Force Advantage.

### Dice

- **10 Dodecahedron Dice (d12)**
- Each die has: **1 white, 1 black, 1 red, 2 green, 3 blue, 4 yellow**
- **Black** = WILD hit (any color, for current Battle Stage)
- **White** = hit only Units in boxes with a white triangle marker

### Flags

- **Jumbo Flags** (on black plastic tokens): 1 round flag per nation for Turn Order Track, 1 square flag per nation for Homeland Status Track.
- **National Flags** (smaller chipboard): for territory control, Stress Track on Morale Board, Trans-Ocean Convoys, Carrier Fighter ownership, Battle-Board unit identification.

## World Map Features

### Regions

- **Territory**: land region with an Information Bar (Region ID, SV, Resources, icons).
- **Sea Region**: blue region with light blue ID code (e.g., A-5).
- **Island**: everything inside an island's circle is a territory. Islands without a Region ID may not be entered.

### Information Bar

Each territory's strip of codes includes:
- **Region ID**: black box for territories, light blue for sea regions. Letter prefix indicates area (e.g., G = Germany, A = Atlantic).
- **Strategic Value (SV)**: white box. Higher SV = more important territory. Also determines stress gained when territory is lost.
- **Resources**: colored numbers. Red = Oil, Blue = Iron, Yellow = OSR.
- **Capital indicator**: National Flag on the left side of the Information Bar.

### Territory Icons

- **Industry** (smokestacks): where new Units can be constructed. Number of smokestacks = SV = max Units that can be constructed at one time.
- **Mobilization** (China-specific): special rules for Chinese Infantry deployment.
- **Port** (anchor): undamaged Ports provide Port Advantage in sea battles. Naval Units can only launch from Port-connected industrial territories.
- **Rails** (train tracks): undamaged Rails allow rapid land movement across connected territories.
- **Trade** (round resource icon): some Neutrals offer resource trade (Global War only). This is NOT resource income.

### Convoys

- **Coastal Convoys**: linked to Ports on the World Map. Always active. Can be raided.
- **Trans-Ocean Convoys**: bear a National Flag and labels for origin/destination territories. Active only when both linked territories are controlled by Nations of the same Alliance.
- **Convoy Clusters**: sea regions A-4, I-1, P-0, P-15, and P-16 where multiple convoy rings connect into one large cluster, raided as a single convoy.

### Narrow Sea Passages

Restrict sea movement for Enemy Naval Commands and Troop Transports (Air Units unaffected). Friendly Nations pass freely. Requirements:

| Passage | Regions | Control Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Panama Canal | A-12, P-7 | Central America (territory) |
| Suez Canal | M-3, M-5 | Egypt |
| Danish Straits | A-5, A-6 | Both Denmark AND Norway |
| Strait of Gibraltar | A-15, M-1 | Gibraltar |
| Strait of Malacca | I-9, P-15 | Malaya |
| Turkish Straits | M-3, M-4 | Turkey (Neutral — neither Alliance initially) |

Note: Gibraltar has a magnifying circle; its Port is NOT connected to A-15.

### Adjacency

- Regions meeting only at a corner are NOT adjacent (e.g., U3 is not adjacent to P-4).
- Sicily's circle overlaps Italy — the two territories ARE adjacent.
- Aleutian Islands and Alaska are NOT adjacent to each other.

### Impassable Regions

- **Sahara** (Africa) and **Himalayas** (Asia): Land Commands cannot enter. Air Units may fly over but not land.

## Setting Up

1. Place **World Map** accessible to all players.
2. **Choose scenario** (1 of 5 — see Scenarios section).
3. **Assign Nations** to players.
4. Place **Jumbo Flags**: 1 square flag per nation on White (Start) Zone of Homeland Status Track; 1 round flag per nation on Turn Order Track.
5. Each Nation takes an **O&P Chart pad** and writes the round number.
6. Place **Resource Chart pegs** at "0" for Oil, Iron, and OSR.
7. Place **Morale Board** nearby. Each nation places 1 National Flag on "0" of the Stress Track.
8. Place **Battle-Board** and 10 Dice nearby.
9. Claim **Nation Storage Trays** with Command Tokens, National Flags, and starting Territory Cards.
10. Insert Territory Cards into Card Holders (Resource-producing cards prioritized).
11. **Place Command Tokens** on World Map per Territory Cards/Region IDs. Tokens without Region IDs are placed later.
12. **Place Units** beneath Command Tokens per Territory Card starting forces (or use Rapid Setup — wait to position Units until Commands enter battle or need splitting/merging).
13. **Place Hotspot** on Solomon Islands (only region starting Embattled, with opposing Alliances present).

## Important Terms

- **Territory Control**: owning the Territory Card. Sea regions are never controlled.
- **Alliance**: Allied Forces or Axis.
- **Friendly**: same Alliance. Cannot attack each other. Includes aligned Neutrals.
- **Enemy**: opposing Alliance, or Neutrals aligned with the opposing Alliance.
- **Unit Category**: Land, Air, or Naval (shape: square, circle, hexagon).
- **Unit Type**: specific color within a Category (e.g., green Armor, blue Artillery, yellow Infantry).
- **Active Region**: has a conflict needing resolution (Hotspot Marker placed).
- **Embattled Region**: opposing Land or Naval Commands share a territory or sea region after a battle. Hotspot flipped to Embattled side. Territory Card flipped to Embattled (reduced income) side.
- **Command Stack**: Units capped by a Command Token. Max 8 Units. No limit on number of Commands in a region.

## The Phases

Each round of play has **7 phases**, executed in order.

---

## Phase 1: Direct National Economy

### Step 1. Check Territory Card Facing

Embattled Territories receive reduced income. If a territory is no longer Embattled or Active, restore its card to normal (face-up) and remove the Hotspot Marker.

**Exception:** All Territory Cards of a Collapsed Nation should be permanently flipped to the Embattled side (reduced income) regardless of current controller. This represents the drain of prolonged warfare.

### China's Restrictions

- China can neither gain nor spend Oil during the game.
- China can only make Artillery Units if it controls an Industrial territory such as Peiping.
- China may not trade at all.
- China may not enter sea regions with Land Commands.
- China may not capture Air or Naval Units.

### Step 2. Tally Resource Income

Each Nation counts Oil, Iron, and OSR from their Territory Cards and adjusts Resource Charts (adding to existing stock). Resources are cumulative. If a track exceeds 20, add the white peg (leaving the original at 20).

Resources are public information. A Nation maintains its Resources until it Collapses.

---

## Phase 2: Strategic Planning

### Step 1. Write Orders and Turn Order Bids

All Nations simultaneously write **TOP SECRET** orders on their O&P Charts. Discussion among allies is encouraged. Time limits may be agreed upon.

**Writing Movement Orders:**
- Write 1 movement order per box (up to 9 boxes, 3 rows × 3 columns).
- Each order: Command ID of the chosen stack, arrow, destination Region ID.
- **Multiple orders may NOT be written for the same Command in the same round.** If accidentally done, only the FIRST instance (reading left to right, top to bottom) is executed.
- **Max 9 written orders** per round. **China and Italy: max 6 orders.**
- Destination may be the Command's current region (patrol).

**Bidding Oil for Turn Order:**
- No limit on Oil bid (except current Oil supply).
- A Nation may bid 0.
- **China must bid 0** (but is included in the random draw among 0 bidders).

**Command Reorganization during Phase 2:**
- Splits and mergers are allowed during Step 1 only (before bids are revealed).
- Splits and mergers are never written as orders.
- All splits and mergers MUST be publicly announced.

Once Step 1 is complete, players may not change written orders or bids.

### Step 2. Reveal Bids and Establish Turn Order

Nations announce Oil bids. In descending order, each Nation selects ANY open slot on the Turn Order Track. Ties are broken by random draw of National Flags.

**Turn Order Rank becomes the universal tie-breaker** when not otherwise specified.

### Step 3. Pay Oil for Bids

All bids must be paid — win or lose. Adjust Oil Resource Tracks.

### Canceling Orders

A Nation may cancel any order during its turn in Phase 3 by drawing a slash through the order. Canceling is usually due to a mistake or preemptive Enemy movements. Partial cancellations are not allowed.

### Reorganizing Commands

Nations may reorganize (split/merge) their Commands within each region only at these times:
- **Phase 1**: at any point
- **Phase 2**: during Step 1 only (before bidding)
- **Phase 3**: only forced splits due to pinning (may split once only, not cascade)
- **Phase 4**: only during Battle Debrief and within the Active region
- **Phase 5**: at any point
- **Phase 6**: at any point
- **Phase 7**: at any point

Rules for all reorganization:
- No Command Stack may exceed 8 Units.
- No limit on total Commands in a region.
- All splits and mergers MUST be announced.
- Merging Units must be the same Unit Category (Land, Air, or Naval).
- If no Command Token is available, a split cannot occur.

### Pinning

A Land or Naval Command trying to exit a region containing Enemy Land or Naval Units must leave behind a number of Units equaling the Enemy Units present within that Category.

- All Friendly Units in the region (under any Friendly Command, including those that moved in previously) count toward satisfying pinning.
- Any excess Units beyond the pinning requirement MUST continue to the destination.
- The owner places a new Command Token on the pinned force.
- Pinning is NOT optional for either party.
- Permission from allies is never required for pinning.

### Plotting Land Commands

- Move to **1 adjacent territory**, OR any distance along a Friendly connected Rail path.
- Cannot enter impassable regions (Sahara, Himalayas) by land.

**Rail Movement restrictions:**
- Cannot enter Enemy-controlled territory (or non-Friendly Neutral), even if empty.
- Cannot enter any territory with Enemy Land Units present (even if Friendly-controlled).
- Territories with Bomb Tokens prevent Rail movement into, out of, or through that territory.
- If Rail is blocked, the order must be canceled entirely (no partial moves).
- Land Commands may exit a Friendly territory (but NOT an Enemy territory) by Rail per normal pinning rules.
- **Yellow Zone penalty** disables Rails for that Nation.

### Transporting Land Commands by Sea (Troop Transports)

- Land Commands at sea are "Troop Transports" — sitting ducks in battle.
- Can move up to **2 regions** if both first AND second moves END in a sea region.
- When going ashore: can only move to **1 adjacent territory**.

**Troop Transport Pinning:**
- Troop Transports never pin Enemy Naval Units or other Troop Transports.
- Troop Transports are pinned by unmatched Enemy Naval Units (regardless of quantity or destination).
- **Exception:** Troop Transports may ignore pinning if they have orders to move ashore to an adjacent Friendly-controlled territory (regardless of Embattled status).

**Troop Transports During Battle:**
- Do not participate in combat (no dice rolled).
- Sunk at end of battle if: Enemy Air or Naval Units are in the region AND no Friendly Air or Naval Units survive to defend them. (Check occurs before Land Air Commands step in Phase 5.)
- If both sides' naval and air forces are completely destroyed, Troop Transports on both sides survive.

### Plotting Air Commands

- Move **1 or 2 land/sea regions** (including over Enemy territory or impassable regions).
- During Phase 5 (Refit & Deploy), Air Commands that moved or fought (marked with Arrow Tags) move up to **1 or 2 additional regions** to land safely.
- Air Commands may NOT move by Rail.
- **Air Commands can't pin nor be pinned.**

### Plotting Naval Commands

- Move **1 or 2 sea regions**.
- Naval Commands may pin Enemy Naval Units or Troop Transports.

### Patrols

For any Troop Transport or Naval Command with multiple possible routes, the choice of path is made during Phase 3: Movement Operations. A destination can be the same as the origin (patrol intent).

---

## Phase 3: Movement Operations

### Step 1. Flip Embattled Hotspots

Flip all Embattled Hotspots (from previous round) to the Active side.

### Step 2. Resolve Movement in Turn Order

Each Nation takes their turn, resolving (or canceling) **ALL ROWS** of their written orders. An opponent reads the orders aloud while the active Nation moves Commands.

- O&P Chart is read left to right, and from row 1 to row 3.
- Until a Nation takes their turn, their orders remain secret.
- All orders must be moved as written or canceled.

**Tag Air Commands:** Place Arrow Tags (red for Axis, blue for Allied) atop Air Commands as they move and/or participate in combat. They must later land in Phase 5.

**Canceling and Illegal Orders:**
- Cancel by slashing through the order box. If an Air Command's order is canceled, it gets no Arrow Tag (unless it participates in combat).
- All players may review orders for legality during this phase.
- Partial cancellations are not allowed.

**Partial Movements and Restrictions:**
- Generally not permitted, except for pinning.
- Also restricted by Narrow Sea Passages and blocked Rail movement.

**Splitting During Movement:**
- Commands can't merge or split during Movement (Phase 3), except forced splits due to pinning.
- Forced splits may only split once (cannot cascade into 3+ Commands).
- If no Command Token is available for a forced split, cancel the order.

### Place Hotspot Markers

Place Hotspots on any **Active** regions as they become apparent:
- Invaded territories (even if only a Garrison Force defends)
- Potential Convoy Raids (no orders needed)
- Naval Unit production conflicts
- Embattled regions persisting from previous round

### Step 3. Carrier Fighter Movement

- Aircraft Carriers have special Carrier Fighters that launch AFTER all other movements.
- No written orders or Command Tokens needed.
- Each Carrier may launch 1 Carrier Fighter (in Turn Order if insisted).
- A Carrier Fighter may be placed: in its parent Carrier's sea region, in an adjacent land territory, or in an adjacent sea region.
- **Carrier Fighters never pin, nor are they pinned.**
- Surviving Carrier Fighters are removed from the World Map after battle (returned to storage). Carriers always have exactly 1 Carrier Fighter at the start of each new round.
- If a Carrier is eliminated but its Carrier Fighter is not, the Carrier Fighter still participates in combat that round. Move only the Carrier to the Casualty List.
- If a Carrier Fighter is eliminated but its Carrier survives, move the Carrier Fighter to the Casualty List.

---

## Phase 4: Combat Operations

### Order of Battles

In Turn Order, each Nation selects an Active Hotspot involving their forces. The conflict is resolved. Repeat until all Hotspots are addressed.

- Remove Hotspots from the World Map as they become resolved (unless they remain Embattled).
- A region should never have more than 1 Hotspot Marker.
- Placement and resolution of a Hotspot is NOT optional.

### Battle Resolution Order

1. **Air Battle Stage** (if Air Units are present)
2. **Raids** (Strategic Bombing Raids and/or Convoy Raids)
3. **Surface Battle Stage** (if Land or Naval Units are present; surviving damaged Air Units may participate)
4. **Battle Debrief**

### Battle Setup

**Step 1. Set Facing of Battle-Board:** LAND or SEA side.

**Step 2. Place Units on Battle-Board:** Allied Forces on right, Axis on left. Move Units from World Map to their respective starting boxes by Type and Nationality. Troop Transports remain on World Map. **Units under construction do not battle and are prone to capture if territory is lost.**

**Step 3. Place Command Tokens on Flags:** Match Command Tokens to printed Flags. Use National Flags for multi-nation forces.

**Step 4. Assign Commanders:** Multiple Nations on the same side choose a Commander (from participating Nations) who makes all battle decisions and rolls all Dice. Disputes settled by Turn Order Rank. In Battle Debrief, the Commander decides which Nation claims captured territory and Medal(s).

**Step 5. Assign Combat Stances:** Each Unit Type has Stance options (e.g., Offense, Defense) with different strengths/weaknesses. Each Stance has an Air Combat Value and a Surface Combat Value. Stances are finalized in Turn Order (Commander's Alliance first) and remain locked for the entire battle. Not all Units of the same Type need the same Stance.

**Step 6. Note Advantages:** Place Arrow Tags for Force Advantage and Port Advantage reminders.

### Force Advantage

- Applies during the **Surface Battle Stage ONLY**.
- The side with more Land or Naval Unit **Types** on the Battle-Board has Force Advantage.
- **If one side has Force Advantage, black and white Dice results are misses for the other side.**
- Equal numbers of Unit Types = neither side has Force Advantage.
- Evaluated ONLY at the start of the Surface Battle Stage. If the balance changes after a batch, do NOT re-evaluate.
- Air Units attacking in Surface Stance do NOT affect Force Advantage count, but their dice results ARE affected by it.

### Cruiser Escort Stance

Each Cruiser in "Escort" Stance gives its Commander the option to take hits (including white or black hits) that would normally be assigned to a Carrier (green) or Battleship (red). The substitution choice is made per hit, one at a time. A damaged Cruiser may still use this ability until sunk. Allies can protect each other's Units with Escort.

### Port Advantage

- Applies in a Port-connected sea region when the side with a Friendly Port-connected territory has **Friendly Naval Units** present.
- Both sides may have Port Advantage.
- Damaged Ports and Ports controlled by Nations in **Yellow Zone** or worse do NOT provide Port Advantage.
- **Benefits:** Roll **2 extra Dice** (observing the 30 Dice limit) for the **Surface Battle Stage** of sea battles if Friendly Naval Units are present. Naval Unit repairs are **free** (instead of 1 Resource per unit).
- Territory control and Port Advantage update immediately after each battle.

### Garrison Forces

- All empty territories have a Garrison Force (including Neutrals whose Defense Force has been eliminated).
- If attacked by **Land forces**, the Garrison Force rolls **2 Dice**. If both Dice match the same color matching an invading Land Unit Type (2 yellow, 2 blue, etc.), 1 Unit of that Type is eliminated (no partial damage, no repairs). **Black results are wild. White results are misses.**
- After the Garrison Force exchange, the territory is captured if any invading Land Units remain.
- Air Commands alone cannot capture a territory from a Garrison Force (no battle occurs).

### Conducting a Battle Stage (Air or Surface)

**Step A. Calculate Dice:** Multiply each Unit row count by its Combat Value for the current Stage. Sum all rows. Max **30 Dice** per Battle Stage per side. Also max 30 per Raid.

**Air Battle Stage Dice:** Count all Air Units + any Land or Naval Units with anti-air firepower.

**Surface Battle Stage Dice:** Count all Land or Naval Units + any surviving Air Units (in Ground or Surface Stance) except those conducting Strategic Bombing Raids.

**Step B. Roll Dice in Batches:** Roll in batches of **10 Dice**. Commander first in Turn Order rolls first batch, then opposing Commander rolls, alternating. Perform Step C after each batch. **No results may be saved for a later batch.**

**Step C. Assign Damage:**

If a rolled Die color matches an Enemy Unit color, it is a hit:
- Move hit Units 1 step closer to "ELIMINATED" on the Battle-Board.
- **A damaged (including lightly damaged) Unit must be eliminated before another in the same ROW can be assigned a hit.** (Units of the same Type but in different Stances are in different rows.)
- Resolve all results of a single color before moving to the next, in this order: **Yellow → Blue → Green → Red → Black → White**

**Black Die results:** WILD — may be used as any color (for the current Battle Stage). May be used to intentionally miss.

**White Die results:** May only hit Units in a box with a **white triangle** marker. If the Enemy has Force Advantage, black and white results are always misses.

**Battle Stage targeting rules:**
- **Air Battle Stage**: only Air Units may be hit (even though Land/Naval Units might have participated in rolling dice).
- **Surface Battle Stage**: only Land or Naval Units may be hit (even though Air Units might have participated).

### Damage States

- **Initial Status**: normal (starting position).
- **Lightly Damaged**: Carriers, Battleships, and Armor have an extra damage box. No repair needed for light damage.
- **Damaged**: red cross icon. Requires repair (1 Resource) or elimination.
- **Eliminated**: removed at end of battle to Casualty List.

**Submarine Dive:** Damaged Submarines in the "DIVE" box are not susceptible to further white hits. After each batch of Enemy Dice, damaged-but-not-eliminated Submarines flee the battle entirely (move to ESCAPED area), avoiding further damage and the need to repair.

### Strategic Bombing Raids

Can occur in any territory with a potential **Enemy** target: Resources, Units under construction (including Naval Units), and/or Infrastructure.

- Bombers conduct Raids between the Air Battle Stage and Surface Battle Stage (if they survive).
- Bombers placed in the "Strategic" Stance on the Battle-Board.
- Each raiding Bomber rolls **4 Dice**. Apply damage before rolling for the next Bomber. Max 30 Dice total per Raid.

**Raid results:**
| Die Color | Effect |
|---|---|
| Yellow | Destroy 1 Infantry or Submarine under construction, OR deplete 1 Enemy OSR |
| Blue | Destroy 1 Artillery or Cruiser under construction, OR deplete 1 Enemy Iron |
| Green | Destroy 1 Armor, Carrier, or Fighter under construction |
| Red | Destroy 1 Bomber or Battleship under construction, OR deplete 1 Enemy Oil |
| White | Damage Infrastructure: add a Bomb Token (disables Ports and Rails, lose 1 smokestack) |
| Black | Choose any 1 of the above results (even if it results in no damage) |

### Damaged Infrastructure

- Bomb Tokens are permanent unless repaired (Bomb Damage Repair rule, p.31).
- Only territories with Industries can have more than 1 Bomb Token. A bombed territory that is captured remains damaged.
- **No Port Advantage** from damaged Ports (but Ports are otherwise functional).
- **No Rail movement** into, out of, or through the territory.
- **Lose 1 smokestack** per Bomb Token (permanently reduces production capacity).

### Convoy Raids

Convoys may be raided once each round (no orders required). They "refresh" each round.

**Identifying Convoy Raids:** If an air or naval force is in a sea region with an active Enemy Convoy AND no defending Naval or Air Units are present at the start of Phase 4, place a Hotspot. The raid must be resolved. Troop Transports do not defend Convoys but may be sunk in addition to the raid.

**Resolving Convoy Raids:** The attacker rolls **1 Die per Air or Naval Unit** in the raiding Command(s). Each Carrier Fighter counts as a Command. Max 30 Dice.

| Die Color | Effect |
|---|---|
| Yellow | All yellow Enemy convoy ships sunk → controlling Nation loses 1 OSR per ship |
| Blue | All blue Enemy convoy ships sunk → lose 1 Iron per ship |
| Red | All red Enemy convoy ships sunk → lose 1 Oil per ship |
| Black | Wild — may be used as any color |

Each Convoy may only be raided once per round, and each Command may raid only 1 Convoy (or Convoy Cluster).

**Resource Loss Limits (Coastal Convoys only):** A territory can't lose more total Resources of each Type than it currently produces (taking Embattled status into account) from a single raid. However, Strategic Bombing AND Convoy Raids in the same round apply independently. Trans-Ocean Convoys are NOT affected by territory-based Loss Limits.

A Nation's Resources can never drop below 0.

### Battle Debrief

After both Battle Stages are complete:

**Step A. Assess Unit Status and Pay for Repairs:**
- **Initial Status / Lightly Damaged**: normal, no repair needed. Move Lightly Damaged Units back to Initial Status.
- **Damaged**: owning Nation may pay **1 Resource** (any type) per damaged Unit to repair. Resources may not drop below 0. Cannot trade to get repair Resources. If declined, move to Eliminated. (Carrier Fighters may also be declined.)
- **Eliminated**: moved to Casualty List.
- **Port Advantage**: Naval Units gain **free repair** if Port Advantage applies.
- Note: Air Units eliminated due to lack of repair still participated in all Battle Stages.

**Step B. Remove Eliminated Units:**
- Move to the Casualty List on the Morale Board, per corresponding Nation and Type.
- When multiple allies are in the same battle, transfer eliminated Units immediately to the correct Nation's row.
- **Carrier Fighters:** eliminated ones ARE moved to Casualty List. However, if a Carrier is sunk and its Carrier Fighter survives, move only the Carrier (not the Carrier Fighter) to the Casualty List.

**Step C. Check for Lost Troop Transports:**
- Unguarded Troop Transports (without a Friendly Naval or Air Unit in the same sea region) are ALL eliminated if any Enemy Air or Naval Units are present.
- Exception: if the only Friendly Units present are Submarines and there are Enemy Air Units, the Troop Transports are still eliminated.
- Enemy Troop Transports' presence has no effect.

**Step D. Return Command Stacks to World Map:**
- Return surviving stacks to the World Map. Optionally reorganize within the region.
- Do NOT return surviving Carrier Fighters to Carriers (they are removed from map; new ones spawn next round).

**Step E. Update Territory Status (Land Battles):**

*Determine control:* A Nation gains control if its Alliance is the only force with at least 1 surviving Land Unit. If both sides have Land Units, or neither has Land Units, control doesn't change. **Air Units by themselves cannot capture or hold a territory.**

*Exchange Territory Card:* If control changes, the former controller surrenders the card. If control doesn't change but Enemy Land Units remain, flip the Territory Card to Embattled.

*Replace/Retain National Flag:* New controller = original controller's color flag (as indicated by map color). If not the original controller, simply remove all Flags.

*Restoring Control:* An ally must restore recaptured territories to the original Friendly controller (per Territory Card) unless the original controller is in Gray Zone or out of the game. Original Enemy territories (once captured) CANNOT be transferred between allies.

*Check for Captured Units:* Units under an Industry Token are captured and deploy under new ownership in Phase 5. China may capture Land Units (including Armor) but never Air or Naval Units (removed to storage).

*Occupied Territories:* Territories with brown borders revert to Soviet Union; territories with yellow borders revert to China, if recaptured.

*Evacuate Enemy Air Commands:* If only Air Commands remain for the losing Alliance, they must land in a Friendly territory during Phase 5 or be eliminated.

**Step F. Assign Stress and Medals:**

*Award Medals:*
- **Capture of a non-capital territory:** 1 Medal (regardless of SV)
- **Capture of a capital territory:** 3 Medals (regardless of SV or victory requirement status)
- Medals may be awarded to any participating Friendly Nation. A Nation receiving Medal(s) needn't be the one taking control. Medals are never awarded for capturing Neutrals.

*Apply Stress:*
- **Loss of territory:** former controller gains Stress equal to the territory's **SV**.
- **Breaking the Soviet-Japanese Non-Aggression Pact:** 6 Stress for the breaking party.
- **Invasion of a Neutral territory:** 1 Stress per invasion (first time only per Neutral), win or lose.

**Step G. Update Hotspot Marker:**
- If the region remains Embattled (opposing Land or Naval Commands on both sides), flip Hotspot to Embattled side (red/blue).
- Otherwise, remove the Hotspot.

## Phase 5: Refit & Deploy

### Step 1. Land Air Commands

All Air Commands marked with Arrow Tags may move up to **2 regions** and must land in a **Friendly territory** (allowable even if Embattled or newly captured in the current round).

- Resolve in Turn Order if any player insists.
- Remove Arrow Tags upon landing.
- Landing movements are NOT written on O&P Charts.
- Air Commands may split, landing in multiple territories.
- If an Air Command has no legal landing, it is eliminated (Casualty List).
- Air Commands, by themselves, may never take control of an Enemy territory after a battle, even if no Enemy forces are present.
- Remove all Carrier Fighters from World Map to storage.

### Step 2. Deploy Units

Skipped on the first round of play (unless Quick Play).

Nations simultaneously deploy newly constructed Units (from Phase 7 of the previous round):
1. Remove Industry Tokens.
2. Create new Command Stacks (same Unit Category only) OR merge into existing stacks (respecting 8-Unit limit).
   - Land or Air Units deploy in the territory where constructed (even if Embattled).
   - Naval Units deploy to a Port-connected sea region (even if Embattled or only Enemy Units present).
3. Bombed Infrastructure reduces deployment limits. Excess Units unable to deploy are sent to Casualty List (owner's choice of which).
4. If insufficient Command Tokens, excess Units become **Deferred Units** (remain under Industry Token). This occurs rarely.

### Step 3. Reorganize Commands

- Nations may merge or split their own Commands sharing a region.
- May happen simultaneously or in Turn Order if anyone insists.
- No Command Stack may exceed **8 Units**.
- Merging Units must be the same Unit Category.
- Store unused Command Tokens.

## Phase 6: Morale

### Convert Casualties into Stress

In Turn Order, each Nation processes its Casualty List:

1. For each Unit Type, multiply the number of lost Units by its **casualty point multiplier** (shown on the Morale Board).
2. Sum all Unit Types to get **total Casualty Points**.
3. Convert total Casualty Points to **Stress Points** using the **Conversion Chart** on the Morale Board.
4. Add the new Stress Points to any pre-existing Stress on the **Stress Track**.
5. Return lost Units to storage.

#### Unit Casualty Point Multipliers

| Unit Type | Casualty Points |
|---|---|
| Infantry | 2 |
| Artillery | 2 |
| Armor | 4 |
| Fighter | 4 |
| Bomber | 6 |
| Submarine | 6 |
| Cruiser | 10 |
| Carrier | 20 |
| Battleship | 20 |

*Example from rules: the loss of 1 Battleship (20) and 1 Bomber (6) = 26 Casualty Points → 1 Stress Point.*

#### Casualty Points to Stress Conversion Chart

| Casualty Points | Stress |
|---|---|
| 0–19 | 0 |
| 20–35 | 1 |
| 36–51 | 2 |
| 52–69 | 3 |
| 70–89 | 4 |
| 90–109 | 5 |
| 110+ | 6 (max from casualties per round) |

No more than **6 Stress Points** may be gained from Casualty Points by a Nation in a single round.

### Resolve Medals and Stress Points

Besides stress from casualties, stress may have been gained from territory losses, Neutral invasions, or a broken Soviet-Japanese Pact. Complete each step for ALL Nations BEFORE proceeding to the next step (done in Turn Order).

**Step 1. Submit Medals to Cancel Stress Points:**
- Each Nation may give up any combination of **Medals** and/or **Civilian Goods** to cancel Stress at a rate of **1 Medal or 1 Civilian Good = 1 Stress Point**.
- Stress Points, Medals, and Civilian Goods may NEVER be traded among Nations.
- Unspent Medals/Civilian Goods may be kept indefinitely.

**Step 2. Evaluate Stress Levels:**
- Each Nation's square Flag on the Stress Track indicates its **Stress Threshold**.
- If a Nation's Stress Points ≥ its Stress Threshold, advance that Nation's Flag to the next Zone on the Homeland Status Track. Reduce the Nation's Stress total by its Threshold value.
- **Repeat** if remaining Stress still ≥ Threshold. A Nation may advance more than 1 Zone per round.
- If a Nation has no Units and controls no Territories on the World Map, it advances 1 Zone.
- If a Nation is already in Gray Zone, it cannot advance further (does not reduce Stress).

**Step 3. Relieve Stress:**
- Each Nation may spend Medals and/or Civilian Goods equal to its **Stress Threshold** to slide back **1 Zone**.
- Max 1 Zone backward per round.
- Cannot go beyond **White Zone**.

**Step 4. Apply Stress Zone Penalties:**
Stress penalties are **CUMULATIVE** and **RECUR** each round.

### Stress Zone Penalties

| Zone | Name | Penalty |
|---|---|---|
| **White** | Acceptable Stress | No penalty (starting position) |
| **Blue** | Labor and Civil Unrest | Pay 3 Resources (any mix), if able, to restore order |
| **Yellow** | Dysfunctional Rails and Ports | All Rails controlled by the Nation (including in captured Enemy territories) are not usable. No trade by sea. Ports controlled by the Nation no longer provide Port Advantage. Ally's Rails still usable if that ally has not reached Yellow. Deployment at those Ports may still occur. |
| **Orange** | Disrupted Supply Lines | 3 fewer orders available. Draw slashes through the last 3 boxes on the O&P Chart. |
| **Red** | Economic Collapse | No new Resources may be added to the Resource Chart. Current Resources may still be spent or lost. |
| **Gray** | Mass Desertion | Transfer Units (any Type, **excluding Units under construction and Carrier Fighters**) from the World Map to the Casualty List equal to current Stress Points. Stress Points are NOT reduced by these desertions. If an Embattled territory loses its last defending Land Unit, control is not lost until the Combat Phase of the following round (a Garrison Force battle will occur unless the controlling Nation collapses first). |

## Phase 7: Production

### Step 1. Mark Starting Resources

Each Nation fills in "Initial Resources" on their O&P Chart from their Resource Chart.

### Step 2. Note Purchase of Units

Nations simultaneously and secretly decide purchases on the Production side of their O&P Chart.

#### Unit Costs

| Unit | Oil | Iron | OSR | Total Resources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Infantry | 0 | 0 | 2 | 2 |
| Artillery | 0 | 2 | 1 | 3 |
| Armor | 1 | 2 | 1 | 4 |
| Fighter | 2 | 1 | 1 | 4 |
| Bomber | 2 | 2 | 1 | 5 |
| Submarine | 1 | 2 | 1 | 4 |
| Cruiser | 2 | 3 | 2 | 7 |
| Carrier | 4 | 3 | 3 | 10 |
| Battleship | 3 | 4 | 3 | 10 |
| Civilian Goods | any 5 total (mixed) | | | 5 |

*Example: 2 Fighters cost 4 Oil, 2 Iron, and 2 OSR.*

**Civilian Goods** cost 5 Resources of any Type (may be mixed). Place new Civilian Goods immediately on the Morale Board's "Medals" box for that Nation.

### Trade with Neutrals (Global War only)

Trade is only permitted during Step 2 of Phase 7, with non-Embattled Neutrals that show a round Resource trade icon on the World Map.

**Exchange Rates:**
| Resource | Trade in blocks of |
|---|---|
| Oil | 2 |
| Iron | 3 |
| OSR | 5 |

Neutrals only accept 1 of the other 2 Resource Types in exchange. For example, Mexico offers Iron but will not accept Iron — only Oil or OSR.

**Restrictions:**
- **Each Nation is restricted to 1 trade per round total** (e.g., exactly 2 Oil for 5 OSR).
- Each Neutral may trade with multiple Nations each round.
- China may NOT trade at all.
- Player-controlled Neutrals (captured) will not trade with the other Alliance.
- A captured Neutral will only trade with its aligned Alliance.
- Pro-Allied Neutrals ("light green" on map) will not trade with Axis (unless captured by Axis).

**Trade Access by Land:** the Nation or an ally must control a non-Embattled territory adjacent to the trading Neutral.

**Trade Access by Sea:** the sea region connected to at least one of the trading Neutral's Ports has no Enemy Naval Commands. The Axis additionally must have a Friendly Naval Command in that sea region (for intimidation), **unless the Neutral is Pro-Axis**.

**Tracking:** Trades must be declared, land OR sea access verified, before Step 3. The trading Nation adjusts its Resource Chart and writes the exchange on the O&P Chart.

### Step 3. Reveal Purchases and Update Resources

Nations exchange O&P Charts for verification. Gather purchased Units. Update Resource Charts simultaneously.

### Step 4. Place New Units

In Turn Order, place purchased Units in controlled **Industrial territories** (even if Embattled or captured from an Enemy Nation):

- Stack all Units under construction in 1 upside-down stack beneath an **Industry Token**, ignoring the 8-Unit limit, combining all Categories.
- **Smokestack limit**: max Units per territory = SV − Bomb Tokens − Deferred Units present.
- Owner chooses which excess Units are lost (returned to storage) if over the limit.
- Naval Units may only be constructed in territories with a **Port** (do not deploy to sea until Phase 5 next round).
- New Units may not move or engage in battle, but ARE prone to Strategic Bombing Raids and capture.

### Chinese Mobilization

- China must deploy purchased Infantry **immediately** in 1 or more Chinese-controlled territories marked with a Mobilization or Industry icon.
- Number of Infantry per Mobilization territory limited by its SV (Bomb Tokens have no effect on Mobilization limit).
- Chinese Artillery must be placed normally in Industrial territories.
- The total number of Units constructed AND immediately deployed in each Industrial territory is limited by its SV minus Bomb Tokens/Deferred Units.

**After completing Phase 7, a new round begins. Check Embattled status changes on Territory Cards.**

## Victory & Defeat

### Victory Check

Your Alliance wins if it has fulfilled the scenario victory requirements (typically capturing a number of Capital territories) at the **end of any game round**. If both sides meet victory requirements simultaneously, continue until only one side does. If all Nations have no Units on the World Map, both sides lose.

### Collapsed Nation Check

If a Nation has **no Units on the World Map** AND is in the **Gray Zone** at the **end of a round**, it is "Collapsed" and out of the game:

- Remove its Resource Chart; remaining Resources are lost.
- Keep National Flags on the World Map until territories are claimed by other Nations.
- If an ally already occupies such a territory with **any** Units (regardless of Embattled), they MUST claim the Territory Card and place their own National Flag (gaining future Resource income). If multiple allies squabble, use Turn Order Rank.
- **Permanently** flip all Territory Cards in the collapsed Nation's color to the Embattled side (even if controlled by another Nation). This represents the drain of prolonged warfare.
- After collapse, a Nation is no longer Friendly to any Nation. Its original territories are treated as Enemy by all. They no longer have Garrison Forces. They may be claimed by any Nation during Battle Debrief.
- Award 3 Medals (Capital) or 1 Medal each time a Nation captures a former territory of the Collapsed Nation.

**Collapsed Nations and Victory:** If a Nation is Collapsed and its Capital is NOT currently occupied by any Units of an ally, that is equivalent to its Capital being captured for victory purposes.

## Non-Aggression Pacts and the Stalin Doctrine

### Soviet-Japanese Non-Aggression Pact

Active for the **Global War** scenario only. Historical 5-year pact between Soviet Union and Imperial Japan.

**Movement Restrictions (while active):**
- Neither party may enter nor pass through territories controlled by the other (even by air).
- Neither party's Units may contribute to pinning the other's Units.
- Neither party may enter a Neutral territory if the other controls it or has Units present.
- The Soviet Union may not move Troop Transports into the Pacific (sea regions prefixed with "P-").

**Combat Restrictions (while active):**
- Neither party may raid the other's Convoys.
- Neither party may participate in a battle that could cause casualties to the other.
- In rare multi-National situations, require two distinct battles to avoid breaching the Pact.
- Neither party may prevent capture of a third-party territory by its presence. If both parties end up in the same sea region or third-party territory, they must co-exist. The offending party must leave next round (or be sent to Casualty List), unless pinned.
- Neither party may provide Port Advantage in battles involving the other party's Units.

**Dissolving the Pact (no penalty):** permanently dissolves if:
- Either party is in the **Red Zone** or worse at the end of Phase 6: Morale, OR
- Either Moscow or Greater Germany is captured or Embattled.

**Breaking the Pact (with penalty):** If either party violates any restriction, the Pact is broken immediately and permanently. Either party may also intentionally break the Pact at will during Phase 6 (Morale) or Phase 7 (Production). Use Turn Order to determine timing if disputed.

- **Stress Penalty:** the breaking party gains **6 Stress Points** immediately.
- **Medal Penalty:** the breaking party receives no Medals for territories captured from the other party for the entire round (still receives Medals from other Enemy Nations).

### The Sino-Soviet Non-Aggression Pact

- Soviet Land Commands may not enter original Chinese territories (yellow-bordered).
- Chinese Land Commands may not enter original Soviet territories (brown-bordered).
- Soviet Air Commands may enter but NOT land in original Chinese territories.
- Permanently dissolves if either China or Soviet Union is in the **Red Zone** or worse at the end of Phase 6: Morale.

### The Stalin Doctrine

Active until the Soviet Union is in the **Red Zone** or worse at the end of Phase 6: Morale, at which point it permanently dissolves.

**While active:**
- U.S. and British Land Commands may **NOT enter** original Soviet territories (brown-bordered), UNLESS they are occupied by Axis Land Units at the time of movement.
- U.S. and British Air Commands may enter but **NOT land** in original Soviet territories, regardless of control or Axis presence.

### Post-Axis-Occupation Policy

If foreign Allied Land Units end up in restricted territories (due to defeating Axis forces) while the Sino-Soviet Non-Aggression Pact or Stalin Doctrine are in effect:
- Such foreign Land Units may remain indefinitely.
- They may also enter other restricted territories that already have foreign Land Units present.
- No additional foreign Land Units may enter from outside these territories.
- If any such territory has no foreign Land Units at the end of Phase 3, it reverts to fully restricted.

## Advanced Optional Rules

All optional rules may be used in **any combination** if all players agree.

### Simultaneous Battles

Resolve multiple Hotspots simultaneously. No single player should be involved in multiple simultaneous battles. Requires a second set of Dice and likely a second Battle-Board.

### Force Advantage Batch Cap

For the Surface Battle Stage ONLY:
- A side with **1 fewer** Land or Naval Unit Type can only roll **2 batches** maximum (20 Dice).
- A side with **2+ fewer** Land or Naval Unit Types can only roll **1 batch** maximum (10 Dice).

### Long-Range Amphibious Landings

Troop Transports may move **2 regions** if: their first move ends in a sea region, their second move ends in a territory, AND at least one of these is true:
- The destination territory is Friendly-controlled (regardless of Embattled status).
- The destination already has Friendly Land Units present (beachhead established).
- The destination has no Enemy Land Units present at the time of entry.

A Garrison Force battle will occur if the territory is Enemy-controlled. The Pinning Exception (page 10) does not apply.

### Bomb Damage Repair

During Phase 7 (after trading but before finalizing purchases), a Nation may pay **9 Iron** to remove 1 Bomb Token from any territory they control. Announced before Step 3 (purchases revealed). Max **1 Bomb Token** removed per round.

## 'Take Her Down' Nano-Expansion

Optional expansion adding 20 Convoy Casualty Tokens / Dived Submarine Tokens (double-sided).

### Convoy Casualties

For each Resource lost in a Convoy Raid, place a **Convoy Casualty Token** on the "SUBMARINE" box of the Casualty List for the Nation controlling the Convoy. Convoy Casualty Tokens are worth **6 Casualty Points** each in Phase 6: Morale.

- Multiple escaped Submarines may split and move to different adjacent sea regions (each needing a Naval Command Token).

- Submarines that do not exercise the dive-move option are treated normally (they may eliminate unguarded Troop Transports, are subject to pinning, etc.).

## Quick Battle Reference

Optional rules designed to speed up combat. Do not transfer Command Stacks from World Map — use dry-erase Quick Battle Charts instead.

**Step A. Record Units:** Each Commander records Unit counts per Type in the center area. Assign Stances only for Bombers in land battles with Strategic Bombing potential.

**Step B. Calculate Dice:** Multiply Units by Combat Value per row for the current Battle Stage. Total per side. Max 30 Dice. Check Force Advantage.

**Force Advantage reminder (Surface Stage only):**
- 1 fewer Land/Naval Unit Type → max 2 batches (20 Dice).
- 2+ fewer → max 1 batch (10 Dice).

**Step C. Roll Dice in Batches:** Roll and resolve each batch. If the last batch has only 1 Die, add a second.

**Step D. Assign Hits:** Match pairs of die results by color to eliminate 1 Unit. Black and white are WILD and pair with any color. Max 1 white result per Unit.

- **Unpaired results** are misses (do not carry over).
- **Battleships and Carriers** require triplets (3 hits) to eliminate.
- **Cruiser Escort Stance** does not apply in Quick Battle.
- **Submarines:** white results cannot be used against Submarines at all. An odd yellow result in a batch means 1 Submarine escapes.

**Step E. Battle Debrief:** No repairs (Units are never in a damaged state). Remove eliminated Units from the World Map.
